Chef Calorie

Calorie and macro tracking by recipe, cooked weight, and serving size

Nutrition values are drawn from a built-in database based on USDA FoodData Central, covering common cooking ingredients with unit-to-gram conversions. Automatic matches are estimates; edit any weight or calorie value to refine them, and your changes are saved.
Data is stored on this device and remains until you delete it.
